# NIH/NHLBI · Insomnia assessment and sleep diary

## Diary

A sleep diary can record sleep and wake times, naps, daytime sleepiness, caffeine or alcohol use, and exercise. Clinical history also distinguishes going to bed, getting up, time taken to fall asleep, awakenings and time needed to return to sleep. Sleep onset and bedtime are different observations.

## Evaluation

Discuss sleep difficulty with a clinician when insufficient sleep affects daily activities. Assessment considers symptom duration, habits, health history and daytime effects; tests may be used to investigate other sleep problems. Timing, stage totals or awakening counts alone do not determine the cause.
