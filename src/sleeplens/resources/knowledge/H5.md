# NHLBI — breathing-related sleep symptoms

Selected paraphrases. Source: https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep-apnea/symptoms . Updated 2025-01-09; checked 2026-11-10.

## BreathingSymptoms

Repeated loud snoring, pauses in breathing or gasping during sleep, particularly with daytime sleepiness, merit discussing with a healthcare professional. These symptoms can occur with sleep apnea; they do not prove it. Assessment may include a sleep study. A duration-only record cannot determine whether these breathing events occurred.
