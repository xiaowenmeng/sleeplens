# NIH/NHLBI · Sleep/wake cycle

## Clock

Internal clocks help regulate sleep timing, with light and darkness acting as environmental cues. People differ in their natural timing: some tend to wake early, others to stay up later. A clock time alone does not establish a circadian disorder.

## EveningLight

Bright artificial light late in the evening can interfere with melatonin release and make falling asleep harder. Screens are possible sources of this light. This does not prove that a particular person's late sleep onset was caused by a screen, or establish one compulsory bedtime for everyone.
