# SleepLens record definitions

Definitions for interpreting the available sleep records. Dataset provenance is maintained in the development documentation and file metadata.

## Fields

`sleep_min` is recorded sleep duration in integer minutes, not a quality score. Detailed records also contain `sleep_onset` and `wake_time` (sleep onset and final awakening, ISO 8601 timestamps with Europe/Berlin offsets), `night_awakenings` (intermediate awakenings, excluding final wake-up), `awake_min` (total awake minutes between onset and final awakening), and `deep_sleep_min`, `light_sleep_min`, `rem_sleep_min` (stage totals in minutes). Stages sum to `sleep_min`; elapsed UTC time from onset to final awakening equals `sleep_min + awake_min`. Stage totals do not provide a timeline or establish clinical sleep quality. Bedtime, getting out of bed, sleep latency, heart rate, blood glucose and diagnosis are not recorded. P01, P02 and P03 identify record profiles, not authenticated accounts. No live device synchronization is available.

## WakingDate

`record_date` is the local date of final awakening, written YYYY-MM-DD. Sleep starting October 18 and ending October 19 belongs to October 19. Detailed onset and wake timestamps include the actual Berlin UTC offset, including daylight-saving changes. Local clock-time averages round to the nearest minute; onset times are anchored to noon on a sleep day so that late evening and after-midnight times are averaged together correctly, while wake times are anchored to midnight. Time-zone travel and separately unrecorded naps are not represented. A waking timestamp is distinct from getting out of bed; sleep onset is distinct from bedtime.

## ProductStages

In SleepLens, `light_sleep_min` represents the combined N1 and N2 stage minutes; N1 and N2 are not displayed separately. `deep_sleep_min` represents N3 minutes. `rem_sleep_min` represents REM minutes, separately from light and deep sleep. These are the product's field definitions, not targets for a healthy stage distribution. Light + deep + REM equals `sleep_min`, excluding intermediate awake time. The records contain stage totals, not their sequence or cycle count. A percentage may be calculated as that stage's minutes divided by recorded sleep minutes, but it is descriptive rather than a clinical threshold.

`night_awakenings` counts recorded intermediate awakenings after sleep onset and before final awakening. It excludes the final wake-up. `awake_min` sums those intermediate awake intervals. Zero means no such event was recorded in these fields; it does not establish that every brief physiological arousal was measured or absent. SleepLens does not provide a validated normality score, diagnose conditions, or infer sleep latency without bedtime data. These definitions do not assert any particular acquisition or device-validation method.

## Validity

`sleep_valid=true` means an available record usable for statistics; its duration is an integer from 0 to 1440 minutes. This is a technical daily-record range, not a healthy range. `sleep_valid=false` requires `sleep_min=null`. An unavailable night or an entirely missing date is not zero sleep. An explicit valid zero is retained but flagged for verification; it does not prove that no sleep occurred.

## Limits

Recorded duration can describe how much sleep was recorded, but cannot establish sleep quality, a disease, the cause of a change or total daily sleep. For an explanation of a possible contributor, use the person's stated routine and relevant sleep guidance, not duration alone.
