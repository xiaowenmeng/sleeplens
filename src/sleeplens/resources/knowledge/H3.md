# NHLBI — insomnia treatment

Selected paraphrases. Source: https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/insomnia/treatment . Updated 2022-03-24; checked 2026-11-10.

## StimulusControl

Stimulus control links bed with sleep: go to bed when sleepy, leave bed if unable to sleep, and return when sleepy. Use bed for sleep rather than prolonged wakeful activities. This is one component of CBT-I, not proof of a diagnosis.

## CBTI

NHLBI describes cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I), commonly delivered over six to eight weeks, as the usual first treatment for long-term insomnia. It includes work on sleep-related thoughts, relaxation, education and behavioral techniques. Support can be in person, by phone or online. Do not turn its sleep-restriction component into a self-prescribed reduced sleep window; clinician-guided tailoring matters.

## Medicines

Discuss insomnia medicines or supplements with a healthcare professional. Antihistamine sleep aids may be unsafe for some people. NHLBI notes that evidence has not established melatonin as an effective insomnia treatment. Do not infer a suitable drug or dose from a sleep record.
