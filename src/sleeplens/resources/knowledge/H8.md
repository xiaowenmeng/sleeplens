# NIH/NHLBI · Insomnia symptoms

## NightWaking

Frequent nighttime waking, difficulty staying asleep, or waking early without being able to return to sleep can occur with insomnia. Feeling unrefreshed, daytime sleepiness and difficulty concentrating also matter. An awakening count alone does not establish a diagnosis; this page gives no universal normal-count cutoff.

## SymptomDiary

Keep notes about sleep difficulties and share them with a healthcare professional. Note how the night affected feeling rested and functioning during the day, rather than relying only on a device's count of awakenings.
