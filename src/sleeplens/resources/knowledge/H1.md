# CDC About Sleep — selected paraphrased reference

Paraphrased from https://www.cdc.gov/sleep/about/ . Page dated May 15, 2024; checked November 24, 2026. This is a short selection, not a complete clinical guideline.

## AdultDuration

CDC lists at least seven hours of daily sleep as a general recommendation for adults aged 18–60. Sleep needs vary with age; this section does not state recommendations for other ages. This is a general daily reference, not a personalized diagnosis.

## Quality

CDC describes quality sleep as uninterrupted and refreshing. Alongside sleep duration, feeling sleepy or tired even after enough sleep and repeated nighttime waking are relevant to understanding sleep quality.

## Habits

CDC suggests consistent bed/wake times, a calm bedroom, and avoiding afternoon or evening caffeine as general habits. These are options, not explanations of why a particular record changed. Persistent sleep concerns can be discussed with a healthcare professional.
