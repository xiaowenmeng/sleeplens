# Google Health · Device-specific sleep stage estimates

## Estimates

Google Health describes Fitbit and Pixel Watch sleep stages as estimates using wrist movement and heart-rate information, unlike laboratory sensors on the brain and muscles. Its light label likely includes N1 and N2; deep likely includes N3. This describes those products, not all wearables or SleepLens's synthetic records.

## Benchmarks

The manufacturer explains that stage distributions vary with factors including age, sleep duration and prior sleep or activity. Its benchmarks describe comparable users, not universal personal targets. Look at patterns over time rather than treating one outlying night as a diagnosis.

## AwakeDefinition

Google Health's awake total can include time before initial sleep, intermediate wakefulness and time after final awakening. SleepLens's awake_min includes only wakefulness between sleep onset and final awakening. These differently defined totals should not be directly equated.
