# NHLBI — healthy sleep habits

Selected paraphrases. Source: https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep-deprivation/healthy-sleep-habits . Updated 2022-03-24; checked 2026-11-10. Adult general guidance.

## Schedule

Allow enough time for sleep. Keep bedtime and waking time consistent; NHLBI suggests limiting weekday–weekend differences to about one hour.

## WindDown

Use the hour before bed for quiet activities, reducing bright screens and intense exercise. Relaxation or a warm bath may help. Keep the bedroom cool, dark and quiet; spend time outdoors and be active during the day.

## Caffeine

Caffeine can interfere with sleep for up to eight hours. Late-afternoon coffee may therefore affect bedtime. Avoid nicotine, alcohol before bed and heavy late meals. Sensitivity varies; eight hours is not a guaranteed individual clearance time.

## Naps

For adults struggling to fall asleep, limit naps or take them earlier in the afternoon; NHLBI recommends no more than twenty minutes. This is general guidance, not a compulsory schedule for everyone.

## ShiftWork

For shift work, protect daytime sleep from light and noise, limit shift changes when possible and keep caffeine to the first part of the shift. Naps and additional sleep opportunity may help.
